The Doctor

Valentino Rossi is one of the most legendary motorcycle racers in MotoGP history. Born on February 16, 1979, in Tavullia, Italy, Rossi is known as "The Doctor" and has become a racing icon due to his charisma, aggressive riding style, and unique celebrations. He has won nine World Championship titles, seven of which were in the premier class (MotoGP/500cc). Rossi won world titles with three different manufacturers—Aprilia, Honda, and Yamaha—a rare occurrence. His greatest success came in the early to mid-2000s, when he dominated MotoGP with Yamaha. The peak of his career was in 2004, when he first moved to Yamaha and immediately became world champion.
